# Open All Night

## 01 · Open All Night
Two insomniacs keep crossing paths in a sleeping town: a convenience store, rooftop ledges, a park. They
start leaving traces for each other without ever trading names. The 24-hour sign is the hook: the only
other thing awake, and what they become while the town dreams. Verse 1's quarters for "a thing you won't
keep" come back in verse 2 as a quarter left on a ledge "for me to keep".

**Style**
```
Melancholic theatrical psychedelic rock ballad, 130 BPM with a measured swaying half-time pulse, low chest-register male vocals delivered intimately in the verses and opening into a wistful, memorable chorus. Warm organ and rounded bass anchor restrained drums; clean, slightly tremolo electric guitar answers the vocal. Build from close, sparse verses to broad layered choruses, with tasteful harmony vocals, vintage tape warmth, and a polished, dramatic mix.
```
